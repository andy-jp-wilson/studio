# Discovery questions

Short conversations, 15 to 20 minutes each, to find the real problems Studio should solve. Ask two or three designers and one or two developers. There are no right answers; stories about real recent work are the most useful.

Tip: ask "can you tell me about the last time that happened?" whenever an answer is general.

## For designers

1. Walk me through the last flow you designed, from the brief to handing it over. Where did it slow down?
2. When you use components from the design system library, what goes wrong or feels awkward? (Detaching, finding the right one, variants, overrides, the older design system?)
3. How do you show interaction design today: hover states, behaviour, loading, errors? What gets left out, and why?
4. What questions do developers or stakeholders come back with after you hand over? Which ones come up again and again?
5. Have you used Claude, or any AI tool, in your design work? What helped, what put you off, and what felt confusing?
6. If one part of your week could take half the time, which would it be?

## For developers

1. Think of the last design you built from. What was missing or unclear, and how did you find out?
2. What do you need to know about interaction: states, behaviour, edge cases? Where do you usually look for it?
3. How often do designs not match the component library (Storybook)? What happens then?
4. What would a perfect handover from a designer include, and in what form (Figma, notes, CSS, a working prototype)?
5. What causes the most back-and-forth with designers?
6. If designers sent you working HTML and CSS prototypes, would that help? What would make them trustworthy?

## After the conversations

Note for each person: the three biggest problems they described, in their words. Then look for problems that came up more than once; those go first in "A day with Studio".
